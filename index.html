How Cloves Found Me

Years ago at the first Southeast Asia e-gaming event, I met Ibnu, an Indonesian official. In the smoking area, he handed me a pack of Dunhill cigarettes laced with cloves. “Only in Indonesia,” he said with a big smile. That warm, sweet-spicy aromatic hit stayed with me. Later I discovered how beautifully cloves dance with ginger, cinnamon, and even fresh herbs like mint, tarragon, or dill. This cookie is my little tribute to that memory.

Memory enters through the nose first.

02

The Flavor Journey

First bite: crisp toasted oat crunch. Then warm buttery softness. Bright ginger zing wakes everything up, followed by the deep sweet-woody embrace of cinnamon and cloves. Roasted black sesame seeds pop with nutty richness while soft chamomile whispers gentle honey-floral notes. Wheat germ adds quiet nourishing earthiness.

A comforting aromatic hug from the pantry — but slightly feral.

03

The Recipe

Bite-Sized Batch
1 cup rolled oats
½ cup flour
¼ cup wheat germ
2 tbsp cassava starch
4–5 tbsp real butter
3–4 tbsp sugar or honey
1–2 tbsp fresh ginger
½ tsp cloves
½–¾ tsp cinnamon
1–2 tbsp chamomile flowers
roasted black sesame seeds
pinch of salt
Procedure / Do Not Panic
  1. Cream butter, sugar and ginger.
  2. Mix dry ingredients and chamomile.
  3. Combine into a soft dough.
  4. Shape into roughly 1-inch bites.
  5. Bake at 175°C for 10–12 minutes.
  6. Cool. Wrap individually in wax paper if you feel ceremonial.
